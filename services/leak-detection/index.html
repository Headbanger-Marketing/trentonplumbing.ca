Questions About the Work

How can I tell whether a repaired leak has really stopped?

A stain may take time to dry, so for your Trenton request it helps to separate old marks from anything that spreads or darkens after the repair.

  • Pin down what the repair addressed

    Once the repair is finished, the respondent should state which pipe or connection leaked, how it was located and whether any opening was made to reach it. Give them the earlier stain locations and the fixtures that were running when you noticed it, so the record shows what has and has not been addressed.

  • Compare a damp mark with the old stain

    Compare any new mark with the earlier stain by position, size and whether it changes after a fixture has been used. A water meter reading noted before and after a quiet period may also help the provider judge whether water is still escaping.

  • Record drying and any openings

    Ask what the completion record says about openings that were made, what was left to dry and what finishing remains for you to arrange. Keep dated photographs of the repaired area. Also ask when the provider would want to hear about a change.
